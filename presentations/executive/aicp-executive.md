# AI Engineering Control Plane
Governed execution for AI-assisted software engineering.
---
## The problem
Fast AI assistance without authority, budget, isolation and evidence creates operational and compliance risk.
---
## The proposition
A human control plane that governs execution, context, memory, security and release readiness.
---
## One glance architecture
Console → Harness → isolated workers; PostgreSQL is canonical; providers sit behind LiteLLM.
---
## Evidence and security
Every stage is bounded and auditable. Scanners and CI remain deterministic authorities.
---
## Economics and evaluation
Budget reservations, physical reconciliation and paired evaluation make cost and quality measurable without inventing SLOs.
---
## Current readiness
35 PASS, 3 BLOCKED, 0 FAILED. Dynamic worker/provider E2E, paired benchmark and critical image findings remain open.
---
## Roadmap
Close evidence blockers, expand live Console integrations, complete Academy and harden visual/accessibility gates.
---
## Decision
Adopt governed execution where authority and evidence matter more than a chat surface.
