# Personas
**Developer** wants to start work and understand the exact evidence behind a result.
**Platform Engineer** needs boundaries, lifecycle, budgets and operational readiness.
**Security Engineer** needs findings, attestations, scanner evidence and residual risk.
**Engineering Manager** needs throughput and human attention without invented metrics.
**Executive** needs the proposition, current certification state and roadmap in one view.
**Contributor** needs reproducible guides for adding workers, gates, policies and retrieval.
